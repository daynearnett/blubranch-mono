## Headline: out-of-network vouch is a fully open door — no connection, no shared workplace, no friction

Nina (w3) has zero connection to Marcus Thompson (a random worker she's never interacted with) and
zero overlapping work history — `GET /users/<marcus>/vouch-context` correctly comes back empty
(`given: null, received: null, suggestions: []`), meaning the app itself knows there's no shared
ground to vouch on. We then called `POST /users/<marcus>/vouch` anyway with a made-up company name
("Totally Made Up Co") and it succeeded outright: `201`, a real `pending` Vouch row created. If
Marcus (or anyone) later taps "confirm" on the resulting `vouch_received` push without reading
closely, a fabricated credential — "Worked together at Totally Made Up Co. Would again." — goes
live on his public profile next to his real, verified-feeling trade history. For a product whose
pitch is peer-credibility ("worked together" attestation, explicitly marketed as more trustworthy
than a generic endorsement), this is the whole feature's trust model with no actual gate on it.
**Severity: LAUNCH-BLOCKER.** The code comment in `vouches.ts` says workplace overlap "pre-fills
... it never gates the vouch, because `WorkPlace.companyName` is free text and real pairs would be
missed" — reasonable logic for *connected* users who at least know each other, but there's no
check here for "are these two people connected at all." Recommend at minimum requiring an existing
connection before a vouch can be filed.

## New finding: a 51-day-old pending connection request just... sat there, un-acted-on, with zero nudge

Checking `GET /connections/pending` as w1, we found a request from Nina (w3) dated **2026-08-18**
— over 7 weeks old — still sitting in `pending` status. w1 had never acted on it (accept/reject),
and nothing in his notification feed re-surfaced it as urgent; the original `connection_request`
push from Aug 18/20 was buried under two months of `daily_task` and `profile_nudge` noise by the
time we looked. We accepted it today (with an empty-body `PUT`, doubling as the issue #1 retest
above) and it went through cleanly — so the data path is fine, there's just no expiry, no re-prompt,
and no visual escalation for a request that's been sitting for weeks. A real "Nina trying to break
into the network" would have genuinely stalled out here if the sim hadn't manually dug through the
pending-requests endpoint. **Severity: SHOULD-FIX** — surface aging pending requests (a periodic
reminder notification, or at least sort/badge them so they don't drown under daily-task pushes).

## New finding: notifications don't reflect that the thing they're about already happened

Tommy's (w2) notification feed had an **unread** `connection_request` titled "Nina Kowalski
extended their branch," dated 2026-08-20 — except `GET /connections` shows Nina and Tommy are

Same root cause flavor as known issue #6 (stale state in a notification), just on connections
instead of vouches. **Severity: SHOULD-FIX.**
