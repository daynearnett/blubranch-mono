## Headline 1 — Missing `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` reconfirmed with hard evidence this time: a live job
record's lat/lng *is* the Chicago fallback point, not just an inferred radius-search miss

Last week's finding (Nov 18) inferred the missing key from radius-search behavior. Today, w1's
`GET /users/me/saved-jobs` returned the full job record for a "Columbus, OH" listing with
`"lat":"41.8848006","lng":"-87.6189584"` — that is the hardcoded `CHICAGO_FALLBACK` point
(41.8781, -87.6298) plus the small zip-derived offset `devOffset()` adds, read directly from
`packages/api/src/services/geocode.ts`. Cross-confirmed by radius search: querying
`/jobs?trade=plumber&lat=39.9612&lng=-82.9988` (real Columbus coordinates) with `radius=50` found
**zero** of the two live Columbus plumber jobs; widening to `radius=500` surfaced one at
`distanceMiles: 275.3` — almost exactly the real Chicago→Columbus distance. **Still unchanged,
still the single highest-leverage open fix** (the PostGIS/Haversine math itself was confirmed
correct back on Nov 18).

**New minor observation:** two of w1's older saved electrician jobs (Nov 4, Nov 11) have
`lat:null, lng:null` entirely — not even the dev-fallback point. Separately, the Nov-11 "Journeyman
Plumber" listing didn't appear in the Columbus radius search at any radius up to 500mi, consistent
with a null/missing geo point rather than a far-away fallback. Not pursued further (the route
doesn't expose lat/lng on `/jobs/:id`, only on the list/saved-jobs shape), but worth flagging:
whatever intermittently fails to even write the fallback point is a second, smaller bug sitting
underneath the main Google-key gap.

## Headline 3 — Job-stats view counter is one request behind the funnel chart it's supposed to
agree with — a new minor finding, not previously caught because the sim had never freshly
"viewed" a job it also owns stats for in the same session

Picked e2's featured plumber listing (`totalViews:0` on `GET /jobs/:id/stats` at session start,
`applications: []`). Had Tommy (w2), a non-owner, fetch the job detail (`GET /jobs/:id`) — the
response he got back showed `"viewCount":0` for that very request. Immediately after, e2's
`/stats` endpoint showed `totalViews:1` — so the event-table write (the thing `/stats` reads) DID
fire on that request, but the inline `viewCount` field returned to the viewer in the *same*
response was stale by one. A second fetch by Tommy showed `viewCount:1` (correctly reflecting the
*first* view) while `/stats` had already moved to `totalViews:2`. So the fast counter on the job
object is consistently one view behind the authoritative event table — cosmetic (nobody's blocked
by it), but worth a punch-list line: an employer glancing at the applicant-dashboard funnel chart
and the job-detail "N views" badge side by side would see two different, both-technically-correct-
but-inconsistent numbers.
