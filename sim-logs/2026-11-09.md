## Headline 1: new finding — crew-post coauthor invites are silently suppressed when the invitee has `notifyMentions` off, with zero fallback signal

Ray (w1) created a fresh crew post tagging Tommy (w2) as coauthor. The `PostCoauthor` row was
created correctly (`viewerCoauthorStatus: "pending"` confirmed on Tommy's own `GET /posts/:id`
read), but **no in-app notification, badge change, or push ever reached Tommy** —
`unread-count` stayed flat at 28 before and after, and the notification list showed no
`coauthor_invite` entry at any point. Root cause, confirmed by reading `services/push.ts`:
`coauthor_invite` shares the `notifyMentions` preference gate (`// Co-author invites are
tag-like events; share the mentions preference`), and when that pref is `false` —
**which it is on Tommy's account** (confirmed via `GET /users/me`; every other notification
pref on his account is `true`) — `sendNotification()` returns before even creating the in-app
`Notification` row. So the suppression is *working exactly as coded*, but the design choice
itself is the problem: a worker who turned off noisy @-mention pings has no way to learn a real
person invited them onto a real post, short of opening that exact post from the feed by chance.
Tommy only found out because this session happened to re-open the post directly. Not previously
flagged in any findings doc or day log — this is a fresh catch, distinct from the known
`viewerCoauthorStatus`-missing-from-`/feed` bug (see Headline 2).
