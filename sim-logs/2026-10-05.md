## New finding: `isFeatured` on job creation is still a silent no-op (confirmed twice now)

Re-tested on e3's fresh Unlimited post (`POST /jobs` with `isFeatured:true, isUrgent:true`):
response came back `isFeatured:false, boostFeaturedPlacement:false`, while `isUrgent:true` round-
tripped correctly. Exactly reproduces yesterday's finding — the real field is
`boostFeaturedPlacement`, and the output's own field name (`isFeatured`) is not an accepted alias
on input. No validation error either time; it just quietly drops the boost. **Severity:
SHOULD-FIX** — now reproduced twice, worth fixing before a real employer (or future client code)
hits it and never notices their "featured" boost didn't take.

weekly job with a 7-day cooldown per user, so either the cooldown isn't being honored, or these are
leftover duplicates from the account's dormant period between sim runs (Aug→Oct) that were never
collapsed/deduped when delivered. Either way, a real user opening notifications to five copies of
the same nag is exactly the kind of thing that reads as broken/spammy. **Severity: SHOULD-FIX.**
