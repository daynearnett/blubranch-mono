## Headline: the "lapsed" subscriptions were never actually lapsed — but looked it

e2 (Castillo Mechanical, Blu/pro) and e3 (Tran Builds Group, Blu Max/unlimited) both came back
`GET /payments/subscription` → `active:true` — good — but `currentPeriodEnd: 2026-09-15`, **19 days
in the past**. A real employer glancing at their next-billing date would reasonably conclude they're
about to be (or already were) dropped, even though the badge still says active. Calling
`POST /payments/subscription/confirm` (force re-pull from Stripe) immediately corrected it to
`2026-10-15` — Stripe *had* auto-renewed both subscriptions right on schedule, the local cache was
just one cycle stale. That strongly suggests the recurring-billing webhooks (`invoice.paid` /
`customer.subscription.updated`) aren't reliably reaching staging, so the DB only catches up when
something explicitly calls `/confirm`. **Severity: SHOULD-FIX** — no money or access was actually
at risk, but the dashboard was lying about the renewal date until manually poked, which is exactly
the kind of thing that'll generate a support ticket from a real employer. No resubscribe was
actually necessary for either employer once confirmed.

## New finding: a vouch sat unconfirmed for 6 weeks because `/vouches/pending` never listed it

w2 (Tommy) vouched for w1 (Ray) back on Aug 20 ("Worked together at Sim Trade Crew. Would again.").
Ray got the `vouch_received` notification (still unread) telling him to confirm it. But
`GET /vouches/pending` for Ray returned `[]` — empty, nothing to confirm — every single time it was
called. Cross-checking `GET /users/:id/vouch-context` (Ray → Tommy) showed the vouch genuinely
existed with `status: "pending"`, and confirming it directly by id
(`PUT /vouches/:id/confirm`) worked immediately and flipped it to `confirmed`. **The pending-list
endpoint is dropping a real, valid pending vouch.** Per CLAUDE.md the mobile app surfaces
"pending-confirm cards on own profile" sourced from this exact endpoint — if it behaves the same on
staging as it did here, those cards never would have rendered for Ray, and the only way he'd ever
know is the notification (easy to dismiss/miss) or someone telling him the id directly. **Severity:
SHOULD-FIX** (confirmation still works if you have the id, but the primary discovery path is dead).
